Syntakt OS 1.41 patch builder

Build a patched Syntakt OS 1.41 file in your browser.

How this works
  1. Pick the features you want.
  2. Choose your OS 1.41 file.
  3. Press Build and download the new file.

This page only makes the file. It does not touch your Syntakt.

The cards below follow the same order. Card 2, "Extra mods from other people", is optional: skip it unless someone sent you a mod file.

What do these numbers mean?

Patch memory is extra room inside the Syntakt (64 KB) where mods keep their code. The Syntakt makes sound in small pieces called audio slices, about 1,500 a second. A processor step is one small piece of work for the chip that makes that sound.
